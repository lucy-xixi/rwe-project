Causal Inference Analysis Report

Step 6 — Propensity Score & Treatment Effect Estimation

Metformin users vs Other diabetes medication users | N = 69

1. Study Design

Observational comparative effectiveness study using propensity score methods to estimate the causal effect of metformin vs other diabetes medications on the composite outcome of diabetes-related complications or hospitalization.

2. Confounder Selection Rationale

Baseline covariates measured at or before index_date (treatment initiation):

  • Demographics: age, gender (female indicator)
  • Clinical: hypertension, cardiovascular disease, obesity, hyperlipidemia, chronic kidney disease

These variables are established confounders of diabetes treatment selection and complication risk.

3. Propensity Score Methodology

Model: P(Treatment = Metformin | Covariates) via multivariable logistic regression (statsmodels GLM, robust SE). Matching: 1:1 nearest neighbor on logit PS with caliper 0.2 × SD(logit PS).

9. Limitations

  • Small sample size limits matching and IPTW precision.
  • Residual unmeasured confounding (disease severity, HbA1c) may persist.
  • Propensity score model assumes correct specification and positivity.
  • Synthea synthetic data may not reflect real-world treatment pathways.
  • Matching discards unmatched patients, reducing generalizability.
Because this is an observational study, causal interpretation relies on assumptions including no unmeasured confounding and correct model specification.
