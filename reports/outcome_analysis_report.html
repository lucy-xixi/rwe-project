Outcome Analysis Report

Step 5 — Synthetic RWE Diabetes Treatment Study

Metformin users (n=49) vs Other diabetes medication users (n=20)

1. Study Population

Adult T2DM patients from Step 2 cohort assigned to metformin or other diabetes medication at index date. Total analytic N = 69.

2. Outcome Definitions

  • Primary composite: First post-index diabetes complication, cardiovascular event, or hospitalization/ED visit
  • Diabetes complication: Nephropathy, neuropathy, retinopathy, or other T2DM sequelae (conditions.csv)
  • Cardiovascular event: IHD, heart failure, stroke (conditions.csv)
  • Hospitalization event: Inpatient or emergency encounter (encounters.csv)
  • Secondary: Encounter count, medication change, disease progression

3. Follow-Up Description

Follow-up begins at index_date (first diabetes medication) and ends at the latest available clinical record (conditions, encounters, observations, medications) or death.

Mean follow-up: 3020 days (99.2 months)

8. Limitations

  • Unadjusted comparisons may reflect confounding by indication (sicker patients more likely to receive insulin).
  • Synthea synthetic data has limited outcome diversity and follow-up patterns.
  • Condition-based outcomes depend on diagnosis coding completeness.
  • Hospitalization events include ED visits; severity is not stratified.
  • Competing risks (death) not modeled in Kaplan-Meier framework.

Interpretation: These results represent unadjusted associations. Potential confounding factors (e.g., age, comorbidity burden) should be addressed using causal inference methods such as propensity score weighting or doubly robust estimation in subsequent analysis.
